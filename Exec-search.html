Chapter One

The Challenge

Elite executive search is a relationship business. But the operational burden of running it had become a research business, a data-entry business, a formatting business — none of which is why the best consultants got into the field.

Where the best consultants' time was actually going

Hunter Executive is an executive search firm operating at the senior end of the market. Their consultants are skilled relationship builders who understand how to place candidates that fit — not just on paper, but in culture, trajectory, and ambition. That expertise is not replicable. The hours it took to build a lead research brief, map a candidate market, score relationship health, and format a client proposal — those were.

Before working with Automagic, the operational reality looked like this: a new prospect came in and a consultant would spend two to four hours manually researching the company, their leadership, their insurance history, their growth stage, before picking up the phone. A new search assignment meant days of market mapping before a longlist existed. Relationship management meant relying on memory and calendar reminders. Outreach meant starting from a blank page every time.

The intelligence was inside the business — in emails, call notes, CRM records, historical placements. It was just inaccessible at the moment it was needed.

Before
New lead research — 2–4 hours of manual research before the first call
Market mapping — 2–3 days to build a longlist for a new search
Proposals — drafted from scratch, disconnected from market data
Relationship health — tracked by memory and calendar reminders
Outreach — written fresh for every sequence, every time
Meeting intelligence — lost in notes, rarely transferred to the CRM
After
New lead research — complete 12-section brief in under 5 minutes
Market mapping — comprehensive longlists built in hours, not days
Proposals — generated from live market data and relevant case history
Relationship health — scored continuously, at-risk relationships flagged automatically
Outreach — personalised sequences generated in the consultant's authentic voice
Meeting intelligence — every call transcribed, analysed, and entered into the CRM automatically

Chapter Two

What Changed

The goal was not to automate Hunter Executive. It was to give their consultants the operational leverage that had only ever existed at firms ten times their size.

A platform built around the consultant, not the process

Automagic designed and built PESA — the Personal Executive Search Assistant — as Hunter Executive's operational intelligence platform. PESA is not a CRM plugin or a generic AI tool. It is a purpose-built system that understands executive search workflows, holds consultant-specific context, and deploys the right intelligence at the right moment.

The foundational principle was simple: consultants should spend their time on relationships and judgement. Everything else — the research, the enrichment, the brief writing, the relationship scoring, the outreach, the market mapping — should happen around them, not instead of them.

Consultants now walk into first meetings with a complete intelligence brief — company context, leadership history, growth signals, likely search needs, and five tailored discovery questions — generated in under five minutes. Cold calls became warm calls.
