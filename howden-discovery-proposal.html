The Landscape
Why specialty insurance brokerage is one of the most consequential sectors for AI transformation — and why the window for first-mover advantage is narrow.
Context

A workflow-intensive, knowledge-intensive business at an inflection point

Specialty insurance brokerage — and Prime Tech sits at the most complex end of the spectrum — is fundamentally an information business. Value is created by assembling the right risk intelligence at the right moment, matching it to the right carriers, and presenting it in a way that serves both client and market. Every step of that process is currently performed by highly skilled people working with imperfect tools.

AI does not change what the best brokers do. It changes how much of it they can do, how quickly, and at what level of analytical depth. The brokers who move first on this will be able to handle more placements at the same headcount, develop deeper client relationships through continuous intelligence rather than annual touchpoints, and build proprietary data assets that widen their advantage over time.

The Israeli tech and VC ecosystem — the heartland of Prime Tech's client base — is growing faster than any traditional brokerage model can track manually. Funding rounds, IPO events, new digital asset regulations, director appointments, and corporate restructurings are all coverage-relevant events that currently surface only when a client calls. They need not.

"The brokers who move first will be able to handle more placements at the same headcount — and build client relationships that compound through continuous intelligence rather than annual renewal conversations."

The Prime Tech position

A unique data position in the Israeli tech insurance market

Howden Prime Tech has served the Israeli technology sector for years — advising startups, scale-ups, pre-IPO companies, and VC funds across professional liability, D&O, digital assets, cyber, and M&A-related coverage. That history represents an extraordinary accumulation of structured knowledge about technology company risk profiles, carrier behaviour, and coverage outcomes.

That knowledge currently lives largely in people's heads and disconnected systems. A Discovery engagement begins by understanding what data already exists, what form it takes, and what could be done with it — before any conversation about technology or implementation.

Chapter 03
What We'll Investigate
The Discovery maps Prime Tech's placement workflow from first intelligence to ongoing client management — examining each stage as a distinct area of inquiry.
Scope

Six workflow areas, examined with precision

The Discovery is structured around Prime Tech's brokerage lifecycle — six connected stages from new client identification to ongoing relationship management. At each stage, we investigate the current state in depth: how information flows, where decisions are made, what data exists, and where the process creates friction or loses value. We do not arrive with prescribed solutions. We arrive with the right questions.

Area 01
New Client Intelligence
How does Prime Tech currently identify and prioritise prospective clients? What signals indicate that a technology company or VC fund has entered a coverage-relevant moment — and how reliably do those signals reach the business development team? We will examine the gap between available market intelligence and what is actually acted upon.
Area 02
Pre-Engagement Coverage Mapping
Before a formal discovery call, business development agents form a hypothesis about what coverage a prospective client needs. How is that hypothesis built today? What information informs it, and what institutional knowledge does it depend on? We will assess the consistency and depth of this pre-engagement analysis across the team.
Area 03
Client Discovery & Risk Intake
The discovery call and intake process is where client needs are formally established. What tools, forms, and processes does Prime Tech currently use? How does information gathered in discovery translate into a structured risk profile ready for placement? We will examine the quality of structured output from this stage and where information is lost or undercaptured.
Area 04
Market Placement & Quote Cycle
Getting quotes from carriers is operationally intensive — each submission formatted differently, each carrier relationship managed separately, each quote response requiring manual extraction and normalisation. We will map this process in full: volumes, time, carrier relationships, submission formats, and the bottlenecks that slow the cycle from risk profile to bound terms.
Area 05
Client Presentation & Decision Support
How does Prime Tech present competing options to clients? What does a coverage comparison look like today, and how well does it support an informed client decision? We will assess the quality and consistency of client-facing materials, the narrative built around recommendations, and how coverage differences are communicated to non-specialist buyers.
Area 06
Ongoing Client Management
Insurance relationships extend well beyond the point of binding. How does Prime Tech manage clients between renewals — monitoring for coverage-relevant changes, identifying cross-sell opportunities, and maintaining relationship quality at scale? We will examine the gap between the annual renewal touchpoint and the continuous advisory relationship the best brokers aspire to.
Horizontal themes

Cutting across all six areas

In addition to the workflow stages, the Discovery will examine three themes that cut across the entire operation: the current state of Prime Tech's data — what exists, where it lives, and what form it takes; the regulatory and compliance context that governs how client data can be used; and the specific demands of the digital assets and VC coverage lines, which carry structural complexity beyond standard tech insurance.
